Is AI Killing Internships?

Jan 20, 2026 • Coach Saab

Entry-level tasks are the first to be automated. Summarizing meetings, writing basic SQL queries, and drafting emails are now done by AI.

ADVERTISEMENT SPACE 1

The New Standard

Companies don't need interns to fetch coffee or do data entry anymore. They need interns who can orchestrate AI agents. If you can use Cursor or Replit to build apps faster than a senior dev, you are hired.

Soft Skills Matter More

As coding becomes commoditized, your ability to communicate complex ideas and manage stakeholders becomes your competitive advantage.

ADVERTISEMENT SPACE 2

Advice for Students

Stop memorizing syntax. Start building products. Have a portfolio of deployed apps, not just a GitHub full of tutorials.
