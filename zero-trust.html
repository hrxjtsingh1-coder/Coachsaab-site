Zero Trust: The End of the VPN

Jan 25, 2026 • Coach Saab

The old model of "castle and moat" security is dead. Once a hacker is inside the VPN, they have the keys to the kingdom. Zero Trust changes that.

ADVERTISEMENT SPACE 1

Never Trust, Always Verify

In a Zero Trust architecture, every request is authenticated. It doesn't matter if you are the CEO or an intern, or if you are in the office or at Starbucks.

Identity is the New Perimeter

We are moving away from IP addresses and towards strong identity verification using biometrics and hardware keys (YubiKeys).

ADVERTISEMENT SPACE 2

Implementation

Start with Cloudflare Access or Google BeyondCorp. Stop exposing your internal dashboards to the public internet.
